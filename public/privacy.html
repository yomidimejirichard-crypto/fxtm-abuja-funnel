Privacy Policy

Traders Hangout with FXTM, Port Harcourt

Last updated: 2 November 2026

What we collect

When you register for the Traders Hangout with FXTM, we collect your full name, email address, phone number and trading experience level. We also record the time of registration and an approximate city based on your network connection.

Why we collect it

Your details are used only to confirm your registration, contact you about this event, and send you event updates, reminders and venue directions by email, phone, Telegram or WhatsApp. They are not used for any other purpose.

Who can see it

Your details are accessible only to the team organising and running this event. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as they are needed for this event and its follow up, after which they are deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the details you gave us, or to stop contacting you. Send your request to fxtmevents@gmail.com and we will act on it promptly.
